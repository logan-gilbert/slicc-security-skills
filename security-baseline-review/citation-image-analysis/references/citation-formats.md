# Citation Formats

This document lists various citation formats that the skill can recognize and analyze. Below are some common citation formats:

## APA (American Psychological Association)
- Format: Author, A. A. (Year). Title of work: Capital letter also for subtitle. Publisher.

## MLA (Modern Language Association)
- Format: Author Last Name, First Name. Title of Book. Publisher, Publication Date.

## Chicago
- Format: Author Last Name, First Name. Year. Title of Book. Place of publication: Publisher.

## IEEE (Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers)
- Format: [Number] Author First Initial. Last Name, "Title of Paper," Abbrev. Title of Journal, vol. x, no. x, pp. xxx-xxx, Month, Year.

## Harvard
- Format: Author Last Name, First Initial(s). (Year) Title of Book. Place of publication: Publisher.

## Vancouver
- Format: Author(s). Title of article. Abbreviated Journal Title. Year;Volume(Issue):Page numbers.

## Turabian
- Format: Author Last Name, First Name. Title of Work. Place of publication: Publisher, Year.
